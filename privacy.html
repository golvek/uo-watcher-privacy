UO Watcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

UO Watcher provides a timer and optional tools for review pages. This policy describes data handled by the extension.

Data stored on your device and synced by Chrome

The extension stores timer and bank state, task counts, work-time statistics, history and preferences in browser storage. Some settings, statistics and trial information use Chrome storage sync and may be synced by Chrome to your signed-in account, depending on your browser settings. License information and validation timestamps are stored locally.

Review content and clipboard

The extension reads content displayed on supported review pages to provide its features. When you use a copy action, it may place text, timestamps, a transcript or an image on your clipboard. Images may be requested from their existing Facebook CDN source to prepare the copy.

Image search

If you choose the IMG search feature, the extension may send a paused video frame to Google Lens or open a Google Lens search using an image URL. Google processes that request under its own privacy policies.

Premium and payments

To validate a premium license, the extension sends the license key and a generated installation identifier to its license service at api.ogd-watcher.workers.dev. Subscription management may send the license key to that service. If you choose to subscribe or manage a subscription, the extension requests a checkout or portal link and opens the service provided. The extension sends a language preference when requesting checkout. External providers may process information according to their own policies.

Controls

You can change settings in the extension and reset tracker data using its Reset All control. Removing the extension removes its associated local browser storage; Chrome-synced data is subject to Chrome's synchronization and data-management settings.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the extension changes.
